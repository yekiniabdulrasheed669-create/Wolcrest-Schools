Admission Requirements

Wolcrest Schools welcomes applications from qualified students. Below are the basic requirements for admission across different grade levels.

General Requirements (All Students)

📋 Application Form

Completed application form with accurate personal and educational information.

🎓 Academic Records

Official transcripts from previous school(s) demonstrating academic performance and conduct.

📝 References

Letters of recommendation from current/previous teachers or school administrators.

✍️ Essay/Statement

Personal statement about educational goals and why you want to join Wolcrest Schools.

🏥 Medical Records

Physical examination report and vaccination records as per school health requirements.

🪪 Identity Verification

Copy of birth certificate and official identification documents.

Grade-Level Specific Requirements

Grade Level Age Range Additional Requirements Entrance Assessment
Grades 1-3 6-9 years Kindergarten completion certificate Readiness assessment (Math, English, Reasoning)
Grades 4-6 9-12 years Previous school conduct report Achievement test (Math, English, General Knowledge)
Grades 7-9 12-15 years School leaving certificate/transcript Competitive entrance exam (Math, Science, English)
Grades 10-12 15-18 years Performance records from last 2 years Interview + Achievement test
Admission Timeline:
  • January - March: Application period opens
  • February - April: Entrance assessments and interviews
  • May: Admission decisions announced
  • June: Admission confirmations due
  • July - August: Orientation and registration

Financial Aid Process:

Students can apply for financial aid by submitting the Financial Aid Form along with supporting documents during the admission process. Decisions are made based on family income and academic merit. Please contact the admissions office for more details.

Submission Instructions

  1. Complete all required forms in full
  2. Gather all supporting documents (transcripts, references, medical records)
  3. Submit application through our online portal or in person at the admissions office
  4. Application fee: $50 (non-refundable)
  5. You will receive confirmation of application receipt within 24 hours
  6. Assessment dates will be communicated within 1 week
