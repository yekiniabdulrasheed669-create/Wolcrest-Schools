Visit Us

Located in the heart of Knowledge City, Wolcrest Schools is easily accessible by public transportation and has ample parking facilities. You can also take a virtual tour of our campus.

Directions: Take the Metro Line 5 to "Education Station" and exit towards the north. Our campus is a 5-minute walk from the station. Ample parking is available in the school parking lot (₹100/day for visitors).

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to commonly asked questions below:

❓ What are the office hours?

Our office is open Monday to Friday from 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Sunday is closed. For urgent matters, you can call our emergency hotline.

❓ How long does it take to get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. During peak periods like admission season, it may take up to 48 hours. Urgent matters are prioritized.

❓ Can I schedule a campus tour?

Yes! Campus tours are available by appointment. Please contact the admissions office at admissions@wolcrestschools.edu or call +1 (555) 123-4568 to schedule a tour.

❓ What if I have an urgent issue?

For urgent matters, please call our main office at +1 (555) 123-4567 and press 0 to speak with the operator, or visit our office in person during office hours.

❓ How do I pay fees?

We accept online payments, bank transfers, credit cards, and checks. For details about payment methods and installment plans, contact our Finance department at +1 (555) 123-4567 ext. 203.

❓ Is there an emergency contact?

In case of emergencies, please call our emergency hotline or visit the office immediately. For student-related emergencies, contact the student affairs office at ext. 204.
